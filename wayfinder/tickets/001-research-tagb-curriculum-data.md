## Resolution

Research completed successfully. Data gathered for:

1. **Belt Color Symbolism**:
   - White Belt (10th Kup): Innocence (no previous knowledge of Tae Kwon-Do).
   - Yellow Belt (8th Kup): Earth (where a plant sprouts and takes root as foundation is laid).
   - Green Belt (6th Kup): Plant's Growth (Tae Kwon-Do skills begin to develop).
   - Blue Belt (4th Kup): Heaven (towards which the plant matures into a towering tree as training progresses).
   - Red Belt (2nd Kup): Danger (cautioning the student to exercise self-control and warning opponents).
   - Black Belt (1st Dan): Imperviousness to darkness and fear; maturity and proficiency.

2. **Pattern Meanings & Metadata**:
   - Saju-Jirugi (10th Kup): Four Direction Punch (14 moves, cross diagram '+', parallel ready stance).
   - Saju-Makgi (10th Kup): Four Direction Block (16 moves, cross diagram '+', parallel ready stance).
   - Chon-Ji (9th Kup): 19 moves, "Heaven and Earth", creation of the world / beginning of human history, cross diagram '+', parallel ready stance.
   - Dan-Gun (8th Kup): 21 moves, legendary founder of Korea in 2333 BC, capital 'I' diagram, parallel ready stance.
   - Do-San (7th Kup): 24 moves, patriot Ahn Chang-Ho (1876-1938), capital 'I' diagram, parallel ready stance.
   - Won-Hyo (6th Kup): 28 moves, monk who introduced Buddhism to the Silla Dynasty in 686 AD, capital 'I' diagram, closed ready stance A.
   - Yul-Gok (5th Kup): 38 moves, philosopher Yi I (1536-1584, "Confucius of Korea"), 38 moves refer to 38th latitude birthplace, diagram represents "scholar", parallel ready stance.
   - Joong-Gun (4th Kup): 32 moves, patriot Ahn Joong-Gun who assassinated Ito Hirobumi, 32 moves refer to his age at execution, capital 'I' diagram, closed ready stance B.
   - Toi-Gye (3rd Kup): 37 moves, scholar Yi Hwang (neo-Confucianism authority), 37 moves refer to 37th latitude birthplace, diagram represents "scholar", closed ready stance B.
   - Hwa-Rang (2nd Kup): 29 moves, Hwa-Rang youth group of Silla Dynasty, 29 moves refer to 29th Infantry Division, capital 'I' diagram, closed ready stance C.
   - Choong-Moo (1st Kup): 30 moves, Admiral Yi Sun-Sin who invented the Kobukson (Turtle Ship) in 1592, ends with left hand attack symbolizing tragic death, capital 'I' diagram, parallel ready stance.

3. **Grade Syllabus**: Detailed breakdown of stance, defense, offense, pattern, and theory requirements per Kup rank.
